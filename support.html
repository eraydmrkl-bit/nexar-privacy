NEXAR Support

Getting started

On first launch, either pair with NEXAR on your Mac (the 6-character code shown on the Mac), or enter a Gemini API key from Google AI Studio and tap "Mac olmadan başla" (start without a Mac).

Common questions

It says the Mac cannot be reached. Make sure NEXAR is open on the Mac and both devices are on the same network or on Tailscale, then close and reopen the app.

The confirmation notification has no buttons. Press and hold it; Approve and Decline appear then.
